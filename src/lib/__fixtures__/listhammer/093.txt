So, you're facing three baneblade chassis.
It's pretty tanky, T13 with 4+ invuln from the Enginseers.
The Doomhammer has 6 bullgryns in it, and the Doomhammer can zoom 18", dump them out, and then let them charge.
All the big tanks can do actions and shoot, and can do actions even if they're in melee. But as they're titanic, they can't use overwatch.

The tanks ignore penalties for shooting into close combat, and can fire blast weapons, assuming the target unit is only engaged with the unit I'm trying to fire with.

The bullgryns are really tanky, and can surge move if shot at by something with a strat (Up to d6")

I have THREE redeploys after we're both fully deployed, and that does let me put stuff in strat reserves.

Important strats;
Strip cover from a target, AFTER a titanic tank has shot and hit it
Full re-rolls for a titanic tank
Drive through walls in move/charge phase
+6 attacks, and 2 ap to the close combat (Still hits on 4's)

Bullgryn d6" blood surge move after being shot at
Bullgryn AoC

It's a list built to require zero skills to pilot it, no thinking just big gun.
